# 05 Data sources

Research done 2026-09-26. **V** = verified by fetching or downloading. **U** = unverified.
Raw downloads are in `data/raw/`, which is git-ignored.

## TL;DR
- **Backbone: RokomariBG** (127k Rokomari books, with summaries, authors and 210k reviews). Best single source, but **non-commercial license**.
- **Premises:** generated by us from RokomariBG flap text, Boighor and Boitoi blurbs, and Wikipedia plots (D-008).
- **Taste signals:** RokomariBG reviews plus BanglaBook reviews.
- **Author table:** RokomariBG authors, linked to **Wikidata** QIDs (CC0).
- **West Bengal gap:** only partly covered through Rokomari. Fill from Boighor, Patra Bharati, BoiChoi and Wikipedia.
- **Eval queries:** Facebook groups (manually or through admins), Quora, Reddit, a survey. **No scraping.**
- **⚠️ Open decision:** research/prototype only, or a commercial product? This decides which sources we can use (see the end of this doc).

## A. Open datasets

### A1. RokomariBG ⭐ primary
- **What:** a Bangla book recommendation benchmark built from Rokomari (arXiv [2602.12129](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12129v2), 2026).
- **Links:** [GitHub](https://github.com/backlashblitz/Bangla-Book-Recommendation-Dataset) · [HF mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DevnilMaster1/Bangla-Book-Recommendation-Dataset)
- **License:** GitHub has none; the HF mirror is tagged **CC BY-NC 4.0**. → Treat it as **non-commercial**. (V)
- **Size (V):** 127,302 books · 16,601 authors · 209,602 reviews · 63,723 users · 1,515 categories · 2,757 publishers
- **book fields:** `book_id` (Rokomari ID), `book_title`, `category_id`, `book_summary`, `isbn` (94k filled), `book_pages`,
  `author_id`, `publisher_id`, `average_rating`, `rating_count`, `review_count`, `book_url`
- **Summaries:** about 60.5k have more than 200 characters and 46.6k have more than 500. This is publisher flap text, so it's copyrighted. Use it only as **input** for writing our own premises.
- **author fields:** `author`, `bio` (3.4k filled), `follower_count`
- **review fields:** `review_detail` (not truncated), `user_rating`, `review_date`, `verified_purchase`, votes, with a proper book→review link
- **Review coverage:** 38.9k books have at least 1 review; 10.3k have at least 5
- **Use for:** catalogue ✅ · premise input ✅ · taste signals ✅ · author table ✅ · co-review pairs ✅
- **Local copy:** `data/raw/rokomaribg/`

### A2. BanglaBook (Findings of ACL 2023)
- **Links:** [paper](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.80/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/mohsinulkabir14/BanglaBook) (the CSVs with metadata) · HF (text and labels only)
- **License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (V)
- **Size (V):** 158,065 reviews · about 31k (title, author) pairs · 13k authors · 1,537 categories
- **Fields:** `Book_Name`, `Writer_Name`, `Category`, `Rating`, `Review`, `Site`, `sentiment`
- **Quality:** reviews cut at 255 characters (median 58); ratings skewed toward 5 stars; about 88% of titles have binding suffixes like "(হার্ডকভার)" to strip;
  about 69k reviews were machine-translated from romanised Bangla or English. There's no book ID, so we join on title + author.
- **Use for:** extra review signal only
- **Local copy:** `data/raw/banglabook/`

### A3. Wikidata: CC0 ✅
- **Query (V):** 10,776 literary works in Bengali; 706 novels (modelled with P7937 form = novel, not P31)
- **How often each property is filled:** author 10.7k · genre 2.5k (noisy, e.g. "film promotion" 724) · publication date 206 · series 5
- **Skew:** Tagore has 2,207 items, mostly single poems and essays imported from Wikisource
- **Writers:** 2,880 people with occupation writer and language Bengali
- **Use for:** **canonical author IDs (QIDs)**, birth and death years, links to Wikipedia. Weak for the catalogue.

### A4. Wikipedia: CC BY-SA 4.0
- **bn (V):** `বাংলা ভাষার উপন্যাস` (191 pages, with per-author subcategories), `বাংলাদেশী উপন্যাস`, `বাংলা ভাষার বই`.
  Walking these to depth 3 gives **342 unique article pages** (list in `data/raw/wikipedia/bn_novel_pages.json`). How many have plot sections: U.
- **en (V):** `Bengali-language novels` ~202, `Bangladeshi novels` ~46, `Bengali-language books` ~166 (recursive)
- **Use for:** premises for the **~300–500 canonical classics**, and award lists for the seed catalogue.
- **License:** a premise generated from Wikipedia is probably a derivative work. Keep it in a separate field with the source URL, and attribute and license that text under CC BY-SA.

### A5. Open Library: CC0
- **Query:** `q=language:ben` gives 46k works (V), but the count is inflated (it includes any work with a Bengali edition).
  Titles are romanised in the library style ("Bāṃlādeśera…"), none in Bangla script; descriptions are sparse catalogue notes.
- **Use for:** ISBN cross-matching and romanised name variants. Low priority.

None of them grants a data license. robots.txt checked 2026-09-26 (V).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Boighor** (ebooks) | ~3k (U) | ✅ Full Bangla blurb | ✅ Bilingual genre tags | Sparse | Open | No scraping clause found | **Good for premises and tags** |
| **Boitoi** (ebooks) | ~15–17k | ✅ Bangla blurb (~150 words) | ✅ Genre and mood tags | 1 featured review | Open | Protects the app and database; no scraping clause | **Good for premises and tags** |
| **Rokomari** | ~200k products | ⚠️ "Summary" is often a book excerpt | 1 category | ✅ Largest (review endpoints blocked in robots.txt) | Book pages allowed | Couldn't read | Already covered by RokomariBG. **Email admin@rokomari.com** for permission |
| Prothoma | ~49k | Often empty | Subject | 0 | Open | Nothing relevant | Catalogue gaps |
| Wafilife | ~93k (mostly Islamic, includes non-books) | Empty | 1 category | 0 | Book pages allowed | Nothing relevant | Low value |
| **Patra Bharati** (WB) | ~743 | ✅ Bangla and English blurbs | – | – | Open | – | West Bengal premises |
| **BoiChoi** (WB) | ~1.7k | ✅ Bangla blurb | Weak | 0 | Crawl-delay 30 | Nothing relevant | West Bengal premises (has a public Store API) |
| BoiChitro.in (WB) | ~25k (mixed) | Often empty | – | – | Open | – | West Bengal catalogue |
| Dey's Publishing (WB) | ~7k (U) | Field exists, often null | Tags | – | Open | "Reproduction prohibited" | Facts only, with care |
| deybooks.com (WB) | ~2k | Bangla blurb | – | – | Open | **Bans automated access** | ❌ Avoid |
| Ananda Publishers | U | U (JavaScript-only site) | – | – | Open | – | Needs a headless browser; low priority |
| Amazon.in | – | – | – | – | **Blocks AI crawlers** | Bans data mining | ❌ Avoid |

**Crawling rules if we collect from stores:**
- Obey robots.txt, crawl slowly (at least 30s delay), identify our bot, and cache pages so we fetch each only once.
- Store **facts** (title, author, year, pages, ISBN, tags) and keep blurbs only as private input for writing our own premises.
- Never republish blurbs, review text or cover images.

### Plan for 100–200 eval queries
1. Ask the FB group admins to either (a) pin a consented "যেমন বই চাই" ("the kind of book I want") post, or (b) share about 150 past
   request posts with the top answers, with names removed.
2. Collect 30–50 queries by hand from Quora, Reddit and Goodreads. Store the query wording and answer titles
   plus the source URL; paraphrase anything we publish.
3. Run a Bangla Google Form survey: "describe a book you want" and "a book you loved, and what you'd read next".
4. Build answer keys from crowd answers, check them against RokomariBG co-review pairs, and have 2–3 readers grade them.
5. Balance across genres; add romanised, misspelled and out-of-scope queries.

## D. Which source fills which field
| Field | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Catalogue (works, editions, ISBN, pages) | RokomariBG | Boitoi/Boighor, Prothoma, BoiChitro.in, Open Library |
| `premise_bn` / `hook` (written by us) | RokomariBG flap text, Boighor/Boitoi blurbs | bn/en Wikipedia (classics) |
| `genres` / `moods` / `themes` (LLM-labelled) | Premise inputs + review text | Store genre tags (Boighor/Boitoi) as hints |
| `origin` | Publisher lookup (Indian vs Bangladeshi publisher) | Author origin |
| Author table | RokomariBG authors | **Wikidata** (QID, years), Wikipedia |
| Series and recurring characters | Titles + Wikipedia | Manual list for the top series |
| Reader signals (ratings, co-liked) | RokomariBG reviews | BanglaBook |
| Seed list of important books | Award lists, Goodreads "Best Bengali Reads" (by hand) | Media best-of lists |

## E. ⚠️ Open decision: license path
Our best sources (RokomariBG, BanglaBook) are **non-commercial**.

| Path | What we can use | What's needed later |
|---|---|---|
| **Research / portfolio / non-commercial** | Everything above, with attribution | Nothing extra |
| **Commercial product** | Wikidata, Open Library, Wikipedia (with CC BY-SA attribution), our own labels | Permission from the RokomariBG authors and/or a data agreement with Rokomari, Boitoi or Boighor. Or rebuild the catalogue from open sources plus careful crawling |

Suggested approach: **build the prototype under non-commercial terms** now. Keep provenance on every field (D-007)
so non-commercial data can be swapped out later, and start talking to Rokomari, Boitoi and Boighor early if going commercial.
